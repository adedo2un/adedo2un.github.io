Digital Banking

A complete onboarding and account experience for a digital-first bank — designed to make identity verification feel safe and fast instead of bureaucratic, and everyday banking feel calm instead of cluttered.

OVERVIEW

Banking that earns
quiet confidence.

Meridian was building a digital-only bank account aimed at freelancers and small business owners — people who needed a serious financial tool, not a flashy consumer app. The brief was to design an experience that felt secure and credible on day one, without the onboarding friction that causes most digital banks to lose new sign-ups before they ever see their first dashboard.

I led the end-to-end design: mapping the verification flow, designing the visual system, and building the core banking screens — account overview, transactions, and transfers.

THE BRIEF, IN THREE PARTS
  • Cut onboarding abandonment without weakening identity verification or compliance requirements.
  • Design a visual language that reads as trustworthy and established — classic, not trendy.
  • Give users a dashboard that surfaces what matters (balance, recent activity) without burying it in navigation.

PROCESS

How the work
came together.

01
Discovery
Audited 6 competitor banking apps and ran interviews with 9 freelancers about why they'd abandoned a bank sign-up before.
02
Flow Mapping
Rebuilt the KYC journey as a single linear path with visible progress, removing two redundant verification steps.
03
Visual System
Defined a navy-and-gold palette and a serif/sans pairing to read as established and credible, not start-up trendy.
04
Prototyping
Built interactive flows in Figma for onboarding, dashboard, and transfers, ready for moderated usability sessions.
05
Testing & Iteration
Ran 5 rounds of usability testing; reworked the verification progress indicator after two rounds of confusion.
KEY SCREENS

A closer look
at the work.

STEP 1 OF 3
Identity Verification, Reframed

The original flow asked for documents before explaining why. The redesign leads with a visible 3-step tracker and plain-language reasoning at every step, so users always know where they are and why it matters.

CORE EXPERIENCE
An Account Overview That Leads With What Matters

Balance and recent activity sit above the fold with no scrolling. Quick actions (transfer, pay, request) are one tap away instead of buried in a menu.

CORE EXPERIENCE
Transfers Without Second-Guessing

Recipient, amount, and a clear confirmation step are isolated on their own screens — reducing the misdirected-transfer errors that came up repeatedly in early usability testing.
