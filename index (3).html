HOW I WORK

My Design Process

01 / DISCOVER
Research & Empathize
User interviews, competitive analysis, and social listening to deeply understand the problem space before touching a tool.
02 / DEFINE
Frame the Problem
Synthesize research into personas, user journeys, and a precise problem statement that guides every design decision.
03 / IDEATE
Explore Solutions
Rapid sketching, information architecture, and low-fidelity wireframes. Volume before polish at this stage.
04 / PROTOTYPE
Build to Learn
High-fidelity prototypes in Figma or Adobe XD, with interactive flows ready for user testing and stakeholder review.
05 / TEST
Validate & Iterate
Usability tests, A/B experiments, and heatmap analysis. Design is never done — only improved.
